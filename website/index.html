Features

Everything for the road in one app

Built around how people actually ride and run in Bohol, where signal comes and goes.

📡

Offline recording

GPS tracking keeps working with no signal. Your activity saves on the phone and syncs when you are back online.

⚠️

Hazard reports

Report potholes, floods, stray dogs and traffic. Other users confirm them, and they clear once the road is safe again.

🔊

Voice and vibration alerts

Get a spoken warning and a buzz as you approach a reported hazard, without looking at the screen.

🏆

Challenges and badges

Create or join local challenges, climb the podium and earn badges from Bronze all the way to Elite.

💬

Community and chat

Share routes, post to the feed, like and comment, and message other riders and runners directly.

🔒

Private by design

The start and end of your routes stay hidden. You can delete your account and data at any time.

Get the app

Install in four steps

VITRAX is shared as a direct download, so Android will ask you to allow it once.

Download

Tap Download for Android and wait for the file to finish.

Allow install

If asked, allow Install unknown apps for your browser. This is normal outside the Play Store.

Install

Open the file and tap Install. If Play Protect warns you, choose Install anyway.

Sign up

Open VITRAX, create an account and enter the code we email you.

FAQ

Common questions

Is VITRAX free?

Yes. Downloading and using the app is free.

Why does my phone warn me when installing?

The app is installed from a file instead of the Play Store, so Android asks you to confirm. Allow installs from your browser and choose "Install anyway" if Play Protect asks.

Does it work without internet?

Recording works offline. Your activity syncs when you reconnect. Features like the feed, chat and challenges need a connection.

Which phones are supported?

Android 10 or newer. There is no iPhone version yet.

How is my location used?

To record your activities and to warn you about nearby hazards. The start and end of your routes are hidden from others. Read the privacy policy.
